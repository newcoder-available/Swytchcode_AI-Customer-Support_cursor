## Problem
Customers ask frequent general questions that do not always map to a single failure mode.

## Symptoms
- Questions about LED colors, warranty, offline duration, and accessories
- Confusion between Hub and Hub Pro capabilities

## Cause
Not a defect — informational FAQ for common product questions.

## Resolution
**LED meanings:** solid green = online; blinking amber = network issue; solid blue = setup mode; slow white pulse = firmware install.

**Warranty:** 24 months standard; keep serial and proof of purchase. Physical damage and liquid ingress are not covered.

**Offline grace:** cloud marks Disconnected after 15 minutes without heartbeat.

**Hub vs Hub Pro:** Hub Pro adds Port B and PoE; firmware channels are shared.

**Battery backup:** optional NE-UPS-1 accessory provides ~45 minutes; not included by default.

**Bluetooth:** used only during initial pairing; normal operation is Wi-Fi to cloud.

## When to escalate
Escalate FAQ-only threads when the customer’s question conflicts with their contract (custom warranty) or when LED behavior does not match the table above after a restart.
